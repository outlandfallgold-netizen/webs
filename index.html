Part card

O is the width across and Y the overall height. B or J is the bore — unused when solid, but still printed. C is the wall at the bottom, which is how a ring's bore is worked out; on a solid part C is the flange thickness and R the hub base, so the two of them count the flange and the hub separately. A and X are the width across the top, R and f the raised face.

The letters filled in print on the sheet and the card. They never override the packing maths — the plan still stacks on the outside diameter, thickness and piece weight.
